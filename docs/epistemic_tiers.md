# Epistemic Tiers

Every application must sort its claims into these three tiers explicitly. The purpose is to make it impossible for an exact identity to be mistaken for a physical result, or for an interpretation to inherit the certainty of the algebra it sits next to.

---

## Tier 1 — Exact mathematics

Follows from definitions and standard algebra. True, checkable, and by itself explanatory of nothing.

- The binary partition identities: $a+b=1$ with $a,b>0$, and $4h^{2}+\chi^{2}=1$
- The Thales altitude $h=\sqrt{ab}$ and the response $R=(1-\chi^{2})^{-1}=1/(4h^{2})$
- The intrinsic crossing $h=a/b$ and its equivalence to $a=b^{3}$, hence $b^{3}+b-1=0$
- Uniqueness of the interior solution, from strict monotonicity of $b^{3}+b-1$ on $[0,1]$
- The sign law: $h>a/b\iff b^{3}>a\iff b>b_{*}$, so the crossing is transversal rather than a tangency
- Coordinate transformations among $(a,b,h,\chi,R,\xi)$, and the supergolden coordinates $(a_{*},h_{*},b_{*})=(\psi^{-3},\psi^{-2},\psi^{-1})$ with $\psi^{3}=\psi^{2}+1$
- The Fisher metric $ds_{F}^{2}=d\chi^{2}/(1-\chi^{2})=R\,d\chi^{2}$, and the universal-denominator / contextual-numerator split
- The algebraic consequence of imposing the crossing under a **declared** embedding — for $a/b=C$, the polynomial $C(1+C)^{2}=1$

Note what is *not* in this tier: any statement that a Tier-1 root is a physical value. The polynomial is exact; its interpretation is not part of the exactness.

---

## Tier 2 — Domain diagnostic

Requires the domain to have done work that the geometry cannot do. All five of the following, not a subset:

- **Independently motivated sectors.** Two channels identified by the domain, with a stated reason they exhaust a fixed total.
- **Derived embedding.** $a/b=F(X)$ obtained from domain physics, with the conventional parts separated from the derived parts.
- **Declared normalization.** Every gauge, slicing, or norm choice stated, and any undetermined overall factor reported and propagated.
- **Physical admissibility.** The landmark located inside the range the domain itself permits.
- **A test that could fail.** A held-out prediction, a family collapse with a reported residual, an independent determination that then predicts a trajectory, cross-domain recurrence under pre-declared normalization, or demonstrated compression.

**Nothing in the synthesis note reaches Tier 2.** The perturbative-GR embedding is schematic and untested; the cosmological embedding is derived but returns a negative. That is stated plainly in the manuscript rather than left for a reader to infer.

---

## Tier 3 — Speculative interpretation

Permissible to state, provided it is labelled. Requires Tier-2 evidence before it can be asserted.

- Claims of a universal mechanism behind the recurrence of the partition across domains
- Claims that the intrinsic cubic is fundamental, or that its root is a constant of nature
- Claims that distinct domains share dynamics because they share partition geometry
- Claims of a sharp physical onset at a landmark, unsupported by domain physics — including any reading of the cubic as the point where nonlinear gravity begins, or where a perturbative expansion breaks down

---

## Why the tiering is enforced rather than recommended

The three tiers fail differently, and aggregating them hides which failure occurred. A result can be exactly right at Tier 1 and vacuous at Tier 2 — the two-body mass-ratio landmark is exactly located and physically empty. A result can have a derived embedding at Tier 2 and still have no mechanism — the cosmological cubic state. Reporting a single verdict on such cases loses the information that matters, which is *which* ingredient is missing.
