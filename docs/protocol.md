# The Five-Step Protocol

Steps 1–4 are constructive. Step 5 is what makes the result scientific rather than decorative.

---

## Step 1 — Identify a genuine constrained decomposition

**What must be supplied.** Two sectors that exhaust a conserved total, an exact operator decomposition, or a definitionally complete budget, for a reason internal to the domain.

**What could go wrong.** The commonest failure is the manufactured partition: two unrelated positive quantities divided by their sum. This always succeeds arithmetically and never means anything. A related failure is a partition that closes only approximately, or only in a regime, without that regime being declared.

**What evidence would be sufficient.** A conservation law, a closure constraint, or an exact algebraic rearrangement that a domain practitioner would recognize independently of any partition consideration. The perturbative-GR case qualifies because the rearrangement of the field equation into a linear operator acting on an effective source is exact and pre-existing. Flat ΛCDM qualifies because the density parameters closing to unity is a constraint of the Friedmann system.

---

## Step 2 — Normalize to $a+b=1$

**What must be supplied.** The normalization, stated explicitly, including every gauge, slicing, or norm choice the resulting fractions depend on.

**What could go wrong.** Silent dependence on a convention. If the two sectors are norms of operators, the norm is a choice; if they are components in a gauge, the gauge is a choice. Suppressing this makes the resulting fractions look more determinate than they are.

**What evidence would be sufficient.** An explicit statement of the choices, and ideally a demonstration that the conclusions of interest are invariant across a class of them. Where they are not invariant, the sensitivity must be reported rather than resolved by fiat.

---

## Step 3 — Derive the domain map $a/b=F(X)$

**What must be supplied.** The functional form of $F$, its derivation from domain physics — a conservation law, an evolution equation, an operator estimate, an equation of state — and a separation of what is derived from what is conventional.

**What could go wrong.** This is where the framework is most easily abused, in two ways. First, $F$ can be chosen rather than derived, in which case see Step 5. Second, and more subtly, $F$ can be *partly* derived: the functional form may follow from physics while an overall normalization does not. If the true relation is $a/b=\alpha C$ with $\alpha$ unpinned, then every landmark location scales as $1/\alpha$, and quoting a landmark to four digits while $\alpha$ is unknown to a factor of two is not honest reporting.

**What evidence would be sufficient.** A derivation that a domain specialist would accept on its own terms, with the undetermined normalization reported explicitly and propagated into the stated uncertainty of any landmark. The cosmological case is the cleanest available: the rapidity identity $d\xi/d\ln s=-\tfrac32 w_{\mathrm{DE}}$ follows from the separate conservation laws and requires neither flatness nor two-component closure.

---

## Step 4 — Evaluate intrinsic landmarks under that map

**What must be supplied.** The solution $X_{*}$ of $F(X_{*})=r_{*}$, and then three separate questions answered separately:

- Does the domain independently distinguish $X_{*}$? (Mechanism.)
- Is $X_{*}$ inside the physically admissible range?
- Is the system observed at or near $X_{*}$? (Occupancy.)

**What could go wrong.** Answering "yes" to occupancy and reading it as mechanism, or the reverse. These are independent properties supplied by different layers, and a landmark can have either without the other. A landmark can also fall outside the domain's admissible range, in which case it is not a diagnostic no matter how exactly located it is.

**What evidence would be sufficient.** For mechanism: a feature of the domain's own dynamics at that state — a critical point, a sign change in a rate, a transition in an observable — established without reference to the partition. For occupancy: a measurement, with its uncertainty, and a check against Safeguard 5 on landmark crowding. Note that a demonstration that no mechanism exists is also a result: the flat-ΛCDM flow has a linear potential, $U'\equiv-\tfrac32\neq0$, so it has no interior critical point at all, and the absence of a mechanism at the cubic state is *proved* rather than merely unestablished.

---

## Step 5 — Require independent justification or falsifiable testing of the embedding

**What must be supplied.** Either a derivation of $F$ from independent domain physics, or a prediction that could fail.

**What could go wrong.** Everything, if this step is skipped. Given any target $X_{0}$ and the fixed $r_{*}$, the embedding $F(X)=r_{*}X/X_{0}$ places the landmark exactly at $X_{0}$. An arbitrary embedding can manufacture any desired threshold, so numerical agreement carries no weight whatsoever unless $F$ was fixed in full before the comparison.

**What evidence would be sufficient** — any one of the following, in decreasing order of practical strength:

1. **A family test with a reported residual.** One structure required to fit a family of trajectories that independent physics says should share it but differ by an external parameter, including held-out members. A construction that reports a residual which could be large is no longer a tautology.
2. **Independent determination followed by prediction.** Fix the structure from an action, a free energy, an equation of state, or a measured response curve, then predict the trajectory and compare.
3. **Cross-domain recurrence under a pre-declared normalization.** The normalization protocol must be fixed before comparison, or rescaling manufactures agreement.
4. **Compression.** A complex description replaced by a genuinely shorter one. If the extracted structure is as complex as the data, nothing has been gained.

**A note on failure.** A domain that fails all four routes has been classified, not defeated. "This domain admits no autonomous partition description" is a positive structural finding, stated in a common language, and it is exactly what the protocol is designed to be able to say.
