# Limitations

What the note does not settle, and what would be required to settle it.

## The embedding is the weak point, and it is weak in a specific way

The perturbative-GR embedding $a/b=C$ rests on a schematic estimate: an order-of-magnitude relation between two operator norms, both gauge- and slicing-dependent. The functional form is motivated, but the overall normalization is not pinned. If the true relation is $a/b=\alpha C$, then $C_{*}=r_{*}/\alpha$, and more generally $F(C)=\alpha C^{n}$ gives $C_{*}=(r_{*}/\alpha)^{1/n}$.

**What would fix this.** A derivation of the sector ratio with its normalization, in a declared gauge, with the norm specified — or a demonstration that some observable is insensitive to $\alpha$. Until then the quoted digits of $C_{\mathrm{crit}}$ are exact as a *partition ratio* and indeterminate as a *compactness*.

## Nothing here reaches Tier 2

No test is performed in the note. The perturbative-GR case is untested; the cosmological case is tested and returns a negative. The framework therefore has, at present, no positive domain diagnostic to its name — which is stated rather than obscured.

**What would fix this.** Any one of the four over-determination routes in Step 5 of the protocol, applied to a domain with a derived embedding.

## The cosmological result is a negative, and negatives are narrow

The proof that the flat-ΛCDM flow has no interior critical point applies to flat ΛCDM. It does not rule out a mechanism in a dynamical dark-energy model, an interacting dark sector, or any setting where $w_{\mathrm{DE}}\neq-1$. Conversely, the information-content argument also applies only inside ΛCDM, where the partition coordinates reduce to functions of $\Omega_{m}(z)$.

**What would fix this.** Extending both statements to $w_{\mathrm{DE}}(a)$ models, where the rapidity acquires curvature and the reduction fails.

## Landmark selection is not systematic

The note works with one intrinsic landmark, the altitude–ratio crossing, plus the apex for contrast. The constraint geometry admits others — the self-dual response $R=2$, the coupling–asymmetry extremum, further relational conditions. There is no principle here for deciding which landmarks are worth testing, and a framework that can generate landmarks freely is exposed to a multiple-comparisons problem that the crowding safeguard only partly addresses.

**What would fix this.** A pre-registered list of landmarks with their conditions fixed before any domain is examined, so that the number of comparisons is countable.

## The Fisher metric is a choice, not a derivation

The information metric is principled where $a,b$ are genuine probabilities, fractions, or compositional coordinates. It is weakest where $\chi$ is a convenient bounded transform of an unrelated variable, in which case the metric and any potential together carry more freedom than the data. The note inherits this caution rather than resolving it.

## Occupancy claims depend on cited parameters

Every statement about where the present universe sits relative to a landmark depends on cosmological parameter values taken from the literature, with their uncertainties. The crowding safeguard shows that current precision cannot separate the cubic root from at least two unrelated nearby constants.

## Scope of the structural reading

The note uses structural compactness throughout and makes no claim about the orbital post-Newtonian parameter. Any extension to binary dynamics would require re-deriving the sector ratio for a time-dependent orbital configuration, which is not attempted.

## What the framework cannot do even in principle

It cannot supply a mechanism. If a domain's dynamics does not distinguish a landmark state, no amount of geometric structure will make it distinguished. The framework's value in that situation is to say so cleanly, and to make the negative result reportable in the same language as a positive one.
