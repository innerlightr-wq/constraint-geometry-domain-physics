# Prior Work Map

The synthesis note is **organizational and synthetic rather than a new theorem paper.** Every equation in it was established elsewhere; what is new is the naming of the two layers, the separation of marker from embedding from interpretation, and the statement of the safeguards in general form rather than case by case.

This page records what came from where. DOIs are in [`../bibliography_status.md`](../bibliography_status.md); three of the eight have no deposit identifier yet, and none has been invented.

### The Two-Face Problem
*A Practitioner's Guide to Bridging Inconsistencies Across Physics*

Supplies the **coordinate translation** layer: the same constrained state described in the bounded coordinate $\chi$, the hyperbolic coordinate $\xi=\operatorname{arctanh}\chi$, and the response coordinate $R=(1-\chi^{2})^{-1}$, with explicit transformations between them. Establishes that apparent conflicts between descriptions of a system may be conflicts between faces rather than between physical claims, and that a divergence in one face may be a boundary effect.

In the present architecture this is the observation that the constraint layer has a well-defined coordinate vocabulary.

### Effective Potentials on the Binary Partition Manifold
*A Two-Face Taxonomy of Domain Dynamics*

Supplies the **Fisher kinetic skeleton**, the universal-denominator / contextual-numerator split, and — most importantly for the present note — the **normal-form caution**: applied to a single known trajectory, the first-integral extraction is tautological, because the free function has as many degrees of freedom as the data. Also supplies the four over-determination routes (family test with reported residual, independent determination, cross-domain recurrence, compression) that Step 5 of the protocol invokes, and the demonstration that a failed family test is an informative classification rather than a defeat.

The present note's static architecture is the direct analogue of that paper's dynamical one: geometry supplies the arena, physics supplies the trajectory.

### The Partition-Intrinsic Cubic
*The Unique Altitude–Ratio Crossing, Supergolden Coordinates, and Algebraic Evaluation*

Supplies the **exact altitude–ratio crossing**: $h=a/b\iff a=b^{3}$, the uniqueness of the interior solution, the supergolden coordinates $(a_{*},h_{*},b_{*})=(\psi^{-3},\psi^{-2},\psi^{-1})$, and the closed forms. This is the source of every Tier-1 statement about the landmark itself.

### The Cubic Compactness Threshold in Perturbative General Relativity
*A Conservative Reverse-Thales Diagnostic of Linear–Nonlinear Operator Balance*

Supplies the **compactness embedding** $a/b=C$, the operator rearrangement of the field equation into a linear operator acting on an effective source, and the cubic $C(1+C)^{2}=1$. Also the crucial disclaimer that the equality of the compactness and altitude roots is algebraic rather than evidential.

### Iterated Similarity Composition and the Structure of the Einstein Field Equations
*A Reverse Thales Construction*

Supplies the **reverse-Thales interpretation**: the reading of structural features of the field equations — uniqueness, conservation, perturbative hierarchy — in the language of iterated similarity with closure. Also states explicitly that the field equations are *not* derived from the geometry, which the present note preserves as non-claim (1).

### Matter–Dark-Energy Balance as a Partition Flow
*Velocity, Acceleration, and Constant Rapidity in Flat ΛCDM*

Supplies the **exact ΛCDM flow**: the identities $d\Omega_{m}/d\ln s=-3\mu$ and $d^{2}\Omega_{m}/(d\ln s)^{2}=9\mu\chi$, the landmark $R_{J}=3/2$ as the acceleration extremum, the model-independent rapidity identity $d\xi/d\ln s=-\tfrac32 w_{\mathrm{DE}}$, the Fisher-gradient representation with its linear potential $U(\chi)=-\tfrac32\chi$, the redshift placement of every landmark, and the information-content argument that the partition coordinates add no predictive degree of freedom inside ΛCDM.

The entire cosmological example rests on this paper. The present note adds only two things: the reading of its two landmarks as *opposite failure modes*, and the correction that the ordered branch requires the assignment $a=\Omega_{m}$, $b=\Omega_{\Lambda}$.

### Altitude–Ratio Relaxation on the Thales Partition Manifold
*The Cubic Compactness Threshold as a Robust Interior Attractor for a Class of Imbalance-Driven Flows*

Supplies the **revised relaxation-class result**: transversality of the crossing with its exact slope, and the theorem that for every continuous positive prefactor the imbalance-driven flow has the cubic state as its unique attracting equilibrium — so that the claim is robustness across a modelling class rather than dynamical selection.

This note supersedes the June 2026 version, which used a prefactor and a framing since withdrawn. Forward citations should use the revision. See [`../bibliography_status.md`](../bibliography_status.md).

### Newtonian Two-Body Gravity on the Thales Semicircle
*A Geometric Diagnostic for Mass-Partition Structure*

Supplies the **mass partition example**: fixed total mass as a genuine partition, the altitude theorem $h^{2}=m_{1}m_{2}$, the identification of the reduced mass and symmetric mass ratio as quadratic Thales quantities, and the coupling–timing asymmetry whereby force is altitude-governed while Kepler timing is hypotenuse-governed. All exact re-expressions of standard relations.

## What the present note adds, stated conservatively

1. **Names the two layers** and states the division of labor as an architecture rather than as a remark attached to a particular calculation.
2. **Separates three objects** that earlier work sometimes ran together: the intrinsic marker, the domain embedding, and the physical interpretation.
3. **States the manufacturing objection in general form** — that $F(X)=r_{*}X/X_{0}$ places a landmark anywhere — so that it applies before the next calculation rather than after.
4. **Identifies two independent failure modes**, meaning without occupancy and occupancy without meaning, and exhibits one of each in a single domain.
5. **Generalizes two safeguards** beyond the papers they came from: landmark crowding, and the information-content check.
6. **Records two corrections**: the ordered-branch requirement in the cosmological assignment, and the structural-versus-orbital compactness distinction.

None of these is a theorem. All of them are constraints on how the earlier theorems may be used.
